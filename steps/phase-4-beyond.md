# Phase 4 — Beyond the Project (Ongoing)

This is where the LLM-for-medical direction lives.

- [ ] **LLMs in medicine:** how transformers work, RAG, medical chatbots, ambient scribes — and why hallucination is a patient-safety issue, not just an annoyance
- [ ] **Read one paper a week:** start with the Microsoft MAI-DxO paper — critique it like a journal club: what was the test set, who were the comparators, what would break in a real ER?
- [ ] **Evaluation & safety:** calibration, fairness across patient groups, FDA/regulatory view of AI as a medical device
- [ ] **Career:** health-tech / AI roles value a portfolio over certificates — this project IS the portfolio. The UK Digital Health & AI Fellowship (~8 weeks, takes clinicians with zero tech background) is a real next step, and a first role can come in 6–12 months
- [ ] **Keep building:** a second project — e.g. the RAG chatbot over your own notes, or a triage model — turns one project into a track record
